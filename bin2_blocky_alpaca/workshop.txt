title=CD: Blocky Alpacas - 方块羊驼 (Companion Dogs add-on, Build 42)
description=Voxel-styled alpaca breeds for Companion Dogs: same animal, same stats, same voice - assembled from boxes.
description=
description=WHAT YOU GET
description=- 4 blocky fleeces: cream, brown, grey, spotted (spotted has random dark patches per individual)
description=- A full species member: same pack capacity (2.2x a dog), same herding, same early alarm call, same herbivore diet, same heat sensitivity.
description=- Works with the regular alpacas: they are the same species, so they breed together and share the fleece moodle.
description=
description=WHY IT IS SAFE TO USE
description=- The geometry is generated entirely by script: 23 cuboids placed on the base mod's own skeleton, each vertex rigidly bound to one bone. No model, texture or animation from any other game is included - the only third-party reference was the design idea "an animal made of boxes".
description=- The skeleton, the 24 animation clips and the animation set are the base mod's own (and are covered by its asset-usage note). Nothing was retimed: a blocky alpaca walks exactly like the alpacas you already have.
description=
description=REQUIREMENTS
description=- Companion Dogs 0.7.4 or newer (base mod)
description=- CD: Alpacas (this add-on needs it for the alpaca species and its sounds)
description=- Build 42.18 or newer
description=
description=SANDBOX
description=- Spawning follows the alpaca spawn multiplier from CD: Alpacas, so one slider controls both look-alikes.
description=
description=NOTES / KNOWN LIMITS
description=- Not yet play-tested in game (offline renders + Lua contract tests only) - see the repository notes for the acceptance checklist.
description=- English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese are included; other languages fall back to English.
description=- Bones are rigidly bound on purpose: that is what makes the blocky look. It also means no soft skinning deformations at all.
description=
