title=CD: Blocky Alpacas - 方块羊驼 (Companion Dogs add-on, Build 42)
description=[h1]CD: Blocky Alpacas[/h1]
description=Voxel-styled alpaca breeds for Companion Dogs: same animal, same stats, same voice - assembled from boxes.
description=
description=[h2]WHAT YOU GET[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]4 blocky fleeces: cream, brown, grey, spotted (spotted has random dark patches per individual)
description=[*]A full species member: same pack capacity (2.2x a dog), same herding, same early alarm call,
description=  same herbivore diet, same heat sensitivity.
description=[*]Works with the regular alpacas: they are the same species, so they breed together and share
description=  the fleece moodle.
description=[*]English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese are included; other languages fall back to English.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]WHY IT IS SAFE TO USE[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]The geometry is generated entirely by script: 23 cuboids placed on the base mod's own skeleton,
description=  each vertex rigidly bound to one bone. No model, texture or animation from any other game is included
description=  - the only third-party reference was the design idea "an animal made of boxes".
description=[*]The skeleton, the 24 animation clips and the animation set are the base mod's own (and are covered
description=  by its asset-usage note). Nothing was retimed: a blocky alpaca walks exactly like the alpacas you
description=  already have.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]REQUIREMENTS[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Companion Dogs 0.7.4 or newer (base mod)
description=[*]CD: Alpacas (this add-on needs it for the alpaca species and its sounds)
description=[*]Build 42.18 or newer
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]SANDBOX[/h2]
description=Spawning follows the alpaca spawn multiplier from CD: Alpacas, so one slider controls both look-alikes.
description=
description=[h2]NOTES / KNOWN LIMITS[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Not yet play-tested in game[/b] (offline renders + Lua contract tests only) - see the repository
description=  notes for the acceptance checklist.
description=[*]Bones are rigidly bound on purpose: that is what makes the blocky look. It also means no soft
description=  skinning deformations at all.
description=[*]Source, build scripts and the full reasoning: see the repository this item is built from.
description=[/list]
