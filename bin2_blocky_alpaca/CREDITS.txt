CD: Blocky Alpacas - credits and asset provenance
==================================================

Geometry (media/models_X/Skinned/BlockyAlpaca_Body.glb)
------------------------------------------------------
ORIGINAL WORK, generated by script: tools/blocky/make_blocky_alpaca.py

23 cuboids are placed on the base mod's own skeleton (one box per bone segment), every vertex is
bound 100% to a single bone, and the bind-space positions are solved by inverse skinning
(v_bind = (D_b)^-1 * p_target) so that the runtime linear-blend skinning reproduces the boxes
exactly at rest. No third-party model, mesh or asset file is used or derived from.

The design idea - "an animal built out of boxes" - is a generic voxel style. No geometry, texture or
model file from Minecraft or any other game is included; see the repository notes for why copying
those assets would not be permissible and how this was deliberately avoided.

Two skeleton edits are baked into the shipped glb (documented in media/scripts/models_blocky.txt):
  * the leg chains and the neck are straightened (translation only) so the boxes read as columns;
  * every animation clip's translation channels are shifted by the same amount, because each clip
    keys translation for every bone and would otherwise override the rest pose at runtime.

Textures (media/textures/Body/BlockyAlpaca*.png, CDPortrait_blocky*.png, CDBlockyHoof*.png)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ORIGINAL WORK, generated by script: tools/blocky/make_blocky_alpaca.py (body atlases, one atlas
cell per box face) and tools/blocky/make_images.py (portraits, icons, poster, workshop preview).

Skeleton and animations
-----------------------
The 55-bone Raccoon skeleton and all 24 Rac_* animation clips come from Companion Dogs and are used
under the permission stated in that mod's asset-usage note. They are shipped unchanged inside this
add-on's glb (the mesh was rebuilt around them); nothing is retimed or retargeted.

Species, sounds and meat item
-----------------------------
Provided by the companion add-on "CD: Alpacas" (CompanionDogsAlpaca), which is a hard dependency:
the alpaca species registration, the 9 synthesized alpaca sounds (CDAlpaca*) and the
Base.CompanionDogsAlpacaMeat item all live there. Reusing them keeps blocky and regular alpacas the
same animal instead of two look-alikes that cannot interbreed.

Tooling
-------
Offline glTF validation, skinning renderer and the Lua contract test live in this repository next to
the sources (tools/blocky, plus the shared tools in bin2_companion_alpaca/tools/alpaca).
