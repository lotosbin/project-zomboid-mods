description=[h1]CD: Alpacas[/h1]
description=Cats were the lookout, dogs were the fighters. This one is the pack animal: alpacas for Companion Dogs,
description=in seven fleeces.
description=
description=[h2]WHAT IT IS[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]A new species for Companion Dogs - not a dog with different numbers. It refuses to breed with dogs,
description=  its own interface nouns and every per-species line are shipped for it, and it has its own body model,
description=  coats, voice set and spawn table.
description=[*]Seven fleeces: cream, light fawn, brown, black, grey, rose grey and the silky Suri.
description=[*][b]REQUIRES Companion Dogs 0.7.4 or newer (API 11).[/b] It does nothing at all without it,
description=  on purpose and without errors in the log.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]WHAT IT DOES[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Pack animal:[/b] 2.2x a dog's saddlebag capacity, 45-55 base encumbrance. Its whole reason to exist.
description=[*][b]Early warning:[/b] the best sentinel multiplier in the family, but the alarm call is loud.
description=  It will draw zombies to you, and the sandbox noise option still applies.
description=[*][b]Herds your livestock[/b] better than anything else in the mod (herding 2.0), and senses better
description=  than a dog (scent 1.2).
description=[*][b]Does not hunt.[/b] It will not chase or point at prey, and hunt XP is off for it.
description=[*][b]Fights badly on purpose:[/b] it kicks a zombie down and holds it there, but it never lands the
description=  killing blow, and it panics earlier than a dog.
description=[*][b]Herbivore:[/b] hay, grass, vegetables, fruit. It will not touch meat, and the food that poisons
description=  a dog poisons it too.
description=[*][b]Climate:[/b] it suffers above 24 C (thick fleece) and is at home below 4 C. Heat costs it stress,
description=  so shave season matters.
description=[*]One cria per birth, breeds only with an alpaca.
description=[*]Found on farms, and rarely in pet stores and vet clinics, with its own spawn multiplier in the
description=  sandbox options (settled separately from the stray dog setting).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]SANDBOX[/h2]
description=CompanionAlpaca > Alpaca spawn multiplier (0 turns alpaca spawns off, independent of the dog multiplier).
description=
description=[h2]NOTES AND LIMITS[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]The alpaca's geometry is Quaternius' CC0 'Alpaca' model (Public Domain, CC0 1.0), re-skinned onto
description=  Companion Dogs' skeleton by a scripted per-bone affine transfer plus an inverse-skin bake, so it keeps
description=  the base 55-bone rig and all 24 clips and no animation is retimed. The CC0 model's own rig and
description=  animations are not used. The base mod's skeleton and clips are used under the permission granted in
description=  its asset-usage note. See CREDITS.txt.
description=[*]Voices are synthesized from scratch (no sampled audio), so they are intentionally plain.
description=[*]Damage/bandage overlay (bandSkin) is not shipped: a wounded alpaca still bleeds and can be
description=  bandaged, it just shows no bandage texture.
description=[*][b]Removing this add-on from a save that has living alpacas loses them:[/b] the engine discards an
description=  animal whose type it no longer knows. The base mod degrades cleanly.
description=[*]Dedicated servers: every player needs the add-on, same as Companion Dogs itself.
description=[/list]
