CompanionDogsAlpaca (CD: Alpacas): Asset Credits & Licenses
==========================================================

This add-on is built on top of "Companion Dogs [ALPHA]" (Steam Workshop 3740052292).
Its own asset-usage note says, verbatim:

  "ASSET USAGE: feel free to build add-ons and new breeds for Companion Dogs and to use
   the mod's art in them, just give proper credit; outside of that, please do not extract,
   repackage or redistribute the mod's content in unrelated projects without explicit
   permission."

This add-on uses that permission, and only that permission. It is not usable without the
base mod, and none of the base mod's content is redistributed as a standalone asset.


Body model
----------
media/models_X/Skinned/Alpaca_Body.glb

The geometry is the **CC0 "Alpaca" model by Quaternius**, obtained from
<https://poly.pizza/m/bCVFD48i2l> (direct CDN file:
https://static.poly.pizza/444228bb-745d-49b2-89ef-cc12805deaa8.glb), stored unmodified in this
repository as `tools/alpaca/vendor/alpaca_cc0.glb` (sha256 in `tools/alpaca/vendor/SHA256SUMS`).

Licence: **Public Domain (CC0 1.0)**. The poly.pizza model page states "Public Domain (CC0)" and its
embedded metadata reads `"Licence":"CC0 1.0"`. CC0 allows commercial use, modification and
redistribution with no attribution required; this add-on credits the author anyway, as a courtesy.

What this add-on does with it (scripted and reproducible, `tools/alpaca/build_alpaca_from_cc0.py`):

  * merges the model's 7 material groups into one primitive;
  * maps its 46-bone quadruped rig onto the base mod's 55-bone Raccoon skeleton (explicit table,
    `CC0_TO_OUR`);
  * computes a global similarity fit (Umeyama) between the CC0 skeleton in an Idle pose and the
    base skeleton's rest pose, then a per-bone affine transfer `T_b = D_b^-1 * G * S_b`;
  * **inverse-skin bakes** the vertices: `v_bind = (Σ w·D_b)^-1 · p_target`, so the runtime
    linear-blend skinning reproduces the CC0 alpaca's shape exactly at rest and animates it with
    the base mod's 24 `Rac_*` clips;
  * generates per-triangle UV islands plus 7 recoloured atlas textures (see below).

The CC0 model's own skeleton and its 26 animations are **not** used and are not redistributed: only
its 2060-triangle geometry (as re-skinned above) ships here.

Skeleton and animation clips (`Rac_*`, 24 of them) are part of the base mod and are used under the
permission quoted above. The transfer pipeline and the pose tuning are original work of this add-on.


Body textures
-------------
media/textures/Body/Alpaca*.png (7 atlases: Alpaca, Alpaca_Fawn, Alpaca_Brown, Alpaca_Black,
Alpaca_Grey, Alpaca_RoseGrey, Alpaca_Suri)

ORIGINAL WORK, generated by `tools/alpaca/build_alpaca_from_cc0.py`. The CC0 model ships no UVs and no
textures (flat per-material colours only), so each triangle gets its own atlas cell and the cells are
painted by material class: the three skin materials become the coat's light/main/dark ramps, while
muzzle, hooves and eyes keep fixed dark/white colours. A procedural crimp field (and a vertical silk
field for the Suri coat) supplies the fleece look.


Portraits, icons, poster, workshop preview
------------------------------------------
media/textures/CDPortrait_*.png (7), media/textures/CDAlpaca_Moodle_Fleece_*.png and
CDAlpaca_MoodFleeceFG_*.png (6 sizes each), media/textures/CDAlpacaHoof_*.png and
CDAlpacaHoofDead_*.png (4 sizes each), icon.png, poster.png, preview.png

ORIGINAL WORK, generated by tools/alpaca/make_images.py. The portraits are offline renders
of the derived model above (tools/alpaca/render_glb.py, original work); the moodle glyphs,
hoof icons, poster and preview are drawn with Pillow. No third-party image is used.


Sounds
------
media/sound/CDAlpaca*.ogg (9 clips: alarm, ambient alarm, hum, spit, whine, death, pickup,
chew loop, drink loop)

ORIGINAL WORK, synthesised from scratch by tools/alpaca/make_sounds.py (numpy oscillators,
formant weighting and filtered noise; encoded to Vorbis with ffmpeg). No sampled or
downloaded audio is used, so there is no third-party licence to observe. They are plain by
design: an artificial animal voice rather than a recording of a real one.


Dependencies
------------
Companion Dogs [ALPHA] (Workshop 3740052292) is required. Everything this add-on extends -
the animation state machine, following, combat, herding, the sentinel, needs, the interface,
taming, breeding, the sandbox page and networking - belongs to that mod and is used through
its documented add-on contract only.


Not shipped
-----------
* bandSkin (the four `_BandFront/_BandBack/_CutFront/_CutBack` texture variants). A wounded
  alpaca still bleeds, gets treated and can be bandaged; it just shows no bandage mark, which
  the base mod documents as an acceptable add-on state.
* The CC0 alpaca's own animation set (Idle/Walk/Gallop/Death/... 26 clips). Only the base mod's
  `Rac_*` clips drive this model.
