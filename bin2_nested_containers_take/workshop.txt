title=嵌套容器 · 单品拿取 (Nested Containers - Take Single Item)
description=联机下从"嵌在板条箱/衣柜/尸体里的包"中拿取物品，支持多选/全拿的批量拿取，并刷新包内视图。
description=
description=[h3]原理（沿用 Picking Meister 的机制）[/h3]
description=
description=原版拿不出来是因为容器地址表达不了嵌套包：ContainerID 写入 containerIndex = -1，服务端 findObject() 解析出 null 后拒绝事务。本模组改走引擎自带的网络动作通道 —— 动作放在 shared/ 并定义 complete()，引擎就会把客户端请求同步给服务端权威执行（LuaTimedActionNew -> ActionManager.createNetTimedAction -> NetTimedActionPacket）。参数只带物品 id + 容器引用，服务端自己按 id 还原路径，不依赖任何容器地址。
description=
description=complete() 里手动 Remove/AddItem 并显式同步；其中 sendReplaceItemInContainer(外层容器, 包, 包) 会把整只包重发给客户端（InventoryContainer 连同包内内容一起序列化），补上嵌套包容器"没有相对广播锚点"的洞。
description=
description=[h3]要求与限制[/h3]
description=
description=* 客户端与服务端都要装本模组
description=* 覆盖"包直接位于可寻址容器里"（板条箱/衣柜/尸体/载具部件/地面/玩家背包），更深嵌套需配合三步法或协议版
description=* 建议与界面模组 "Nested Containers - Complete" 一起用
description=
description=[h3]English[/h3]
description=
description=Take a single item out of a bag that sits inside a crate/wardrobe/corpse in multiplayer. Uses the engine's network timed-action channel (a shared action that defines complete() is automatically requested from the client and executed authoritatively on the server), resolves the container by item id instead of ContainerID, and forces a whole-bag refresh with sendReplaceItemInContainer. Must be installed on clients and on the server. Covers the common single-level nesting case.
